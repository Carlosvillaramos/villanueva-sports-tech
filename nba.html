Depth and Hunger for Glory: Knicks Sign Tony Bradley

Knicks sign vet big Tony Bradley to camp deal pic.twitter.com/CUN9JBOU5O

— New York Basketball (@NBA_NewYork) September 30, 2026

The New York Knicks have just signed center Tony Bradley to an Exhibit 9 contract.

This clearly shows that the team from the Big Apple is not satisfied with the title they won last season, but continues to look for pieces to get stronger in every position ahead of the 2026-2027 season, avoiding struggles with injuries or unexpected roster changes that could derail the scheme.

Looking at it from a Knicks fan's perspective, I feel that Bradley still has a lot to give at 28 years old and can be very useful for us.

Plus, it's an important challenge for him, as he has the opportunity to prove the value of this contract signed with the Orange and Blue and help the team fight to repeat as champions.

What he brings on the court:

Bradley, 28 (6'10", 247 lbs), split time between the Pacers and Hawks last season after signing with Atlanta in April.

He appeared in 41 games (4 starts) averaging 4.0 points and 2.8 rebounds in 11.0 minutes.

Over his eight-year NBA career (wearing jerseys for Utah, Philadelphia, Oklahoma City, Chicago, Indiana, and Atlanta), he has logged 234 appearances, averaging 4.3 points (61.8% shooting from the field) and 3.8 rebounds in 10.9 minutes.
